About Train Mate

Train Mate is a simple fitness website that helps users discover exercises and create a workout based on their training preferences.

Our Purpose

Starting a workout can feel complicated when there are too many exercises and training options to choose from. Train Mate makes the process simpler by providing an easy exercise library and a simple workout generator.

How the Workout Generator Works

Train Mate uses simple rules to create a workout based on three main choices.

Training Goal

Your goal determines whether the workout focuses on strength training or cardio.

Experience Level

Your experience level helps select an additional exercise for the workout.

Workout Duration

Your available time changes the number of sets or the time spent on cardio exercises.
